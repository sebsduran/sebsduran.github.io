Media Manager

Media Manager is a private tool that I, Sebastian Duran, use to manage my own channel, Sebs Duran (@SebsDuran), a music reaction channel. I am its only user. It is not a public product, it is not sold or offered to anyone, and it only works on my own channel after I sign in with my own Google account.

What it does

  • Reads my own catalog of videos and playlists so I can plan changes.
  • Updates titles, descriptions and tags on my own videos, including chapter timestamps in descriptions.
  • Reads the caption tracks of my own videos to build those chapter timestamps.
  • Changes my own unlisted videos to public on a schedule I choose.
  • Creates and organizes series playlists on my channel.
  • Reads comments on my own videos to collect song requests from my viewers.

I review and approve changes before they are written to my channel. The tool does not upload videos, does not download video or audio, does not post comments, and does not touch any other channel.

Works with YouTube. Media Manager uses YouTube API Services to read and edit my own channel.
